Who We Are

Developers first. Syndicators because the model earned it.

Assemble Capital is a Los Angeles real estate syndication and development firm. We invest in value-add and opportunistic residential projects through two core strategies: SB 684 / SB 1123 small-lot development, building new fee-simple homes on infill lots under California’s ministerial small-lot laws, and luxury residential development in the neighborhoods where the principals built their record.

Investors participate passively, through project-specific LLCs. The same three principals source, underwrite, entitle, build and sell every project, and they personally guarantee the loans, so investors never sign for the debt.

The track record behind the platform was built by the principals through Thornton Development Group and affiliated companies, which are separate from Assemble Capital. Those projects were not Assemble Capital offerings and did not involve Assemble Capital investors. The principals’ roles in each company are disclosed in every project’s private placement memorandum.

RC Thornton

Partner · Construction & Development

A distinguished luxury developer who has built some of the Hollywood Hills’ most impressive homes, and an early participant in SB 684 development.

Roy Carson “RC” Thornton has completed more than $100 million in developments over his career. Known for exceptional craftsmanship and visionary design, he has built notable homes in the Hollywood Hills, including 7115 Macapa, 7123 Macapa, 7932 Woodrow Wilson and 8070 Laurelmont / Oceanus.

More recently, RC has expanded into multifamily across Los Angeles, applying his luxury-home experience to cost-effective new-construction rental housing. Through his agency practice he has also sold more than $300 million in real estate.

RC is Co-CEO of Hollywood Estates and, with Oliver Thornton, leads Thornton Development Group, the development firm through which they acquire, entitle, design and build luxury single-family and small-lot residential projects in Los Angeles. Its model is comparable to larger infill home builders, with a more luxurious end product, higher-quality finishes, and the ability to build on hillside sites as well as flat infill lots. Thornton Development Group has completed or managed approximately 21 projects since 2007.

RC is a California-licensed general contractor, providing general contracting through Thornton Design Group Inc. (CSLB License No. 1159219). He oversees construction on the firm’s projects, including budgeting, scheduling, subcontractor management and quality control.

He has been an early participant in SB 684 development in the Los Angeles area. Through Thornton Holdings LLC, which he owns and manages, he is developing 1949 17th Street in Santa Monica (“SAMO IX”), a nine-home fee-simple SB 684 subdivision with an approved tentative tract map, for which he obtained a construction loan of approximately $7,100,000 in May 2026, and he serves as its general contractor. Thornton Holdings LLC also owns a stabilized 14-unit multifamily property in Los Angeles. RC has personally guaranteed the construction financing on the principals’ projects, including The Culver VI and SAMO IX.

Oliver Thornton

Partner · Syndications, Investor Relations, Financing & Strategy

A Los Angeles broker and syndicator who leads strategy, acquisitions, capital raising and construction financing across the platform.

Born into a family of real estate and interior design professionals in Los Angeles, Oliver has worked in the industry since he was young. He earned his real estate license in 2015 and his broker’s license in 2019, built a clientele of high-net-worth buyers and sellers, and holds several sales records, including the highest sale in Outpost Estates / Hollywood Hills West in 2020: $7,250,000 at 7123 Macapa Drive.

Oliver is Co-CEO of Hollywood Estates, a luxury brokerage serving the Hollywood Hills and surrounding markets; a principal of Thornton Development Group, which he leads with RC Thornton; and a principal of Assemble Capital, which he leads with Erik Lim. Through Assemble Capital he has sponsored private offerings for The Berryman Residence and the SB 684 projects The Culver VI, Harter V and The Helms VI. The Culver VI was, to our knowledge, the first SB 684 application submitted to the City of Culver City, and Oliver led its construction financing.

He is also expanding the firm’s reach into the South Bay, Orange County, Santa Barbara and Montecito markets.

Alignment & Governance

We Sign for the Debt.
You Get the Reporting.

Sponsor Alignment

The principals personally guarantee the loans on every Assemble Capital project and provide completion support. Investors never sign personal guarantees on project financing; the debt obligations sit with us, not with them.

Economics

Each project is its own LLC with its own investors and terms, set out in that project's offering documents and shared privately with investors we know.

Governance

Manager-managed, project-specific LLCs; a three-principal investment committee; capital-call procedures, major-decision rights, and valuation policy per each operating agreement.

Investor Reporting

Quarterly investor reports, monthly construction updates where appropriate, budget-to-actual and schedule reporting, annual K-1s, and a secure investor portal with source documents.
