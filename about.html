Who We Are

A development firm first. A capital platform because the model earned it.

Assemble Capital is a comprehensive Los Angeles real estate firm focused on generating asymmetric returns through single-family redevelopment, ground-up multifamily, fee-simple subdivisions, and TIC housing. Every aspect of the development process is coordinated by the principals — from acquisitions and entitlements through permits, construction, and the final sale or lease.

The track record behind the platform was built by the principals through Thornton Development Group and affiliated entities: 21 completed projects since 2005, with no notices of default, foreclosures, or lender workouts in roughly twenty years of operating through multiple market cycles.

Assemble Capital is a separate company with its own investors, project-specific LLCs, and offering documents. Development on Assemble Capital projects is directed by its managers, RC and Oliver Thornton, in that capacity. The same principals separately operate Thornton Development Group, an independent company with its own development ventures. Assemble Capital and Thornton Development Group are distinct companies; the principals' roles in both are disclosed in each project's private placement memorandum. The project experience described above was earned by the principals through those separate entities, not through Assemble Capital offerings.

Alignment & Governance

Structured like we're the investor. Because we are.

Sponsor Alignment

GP co-investment in all seven active deals, with the principals providing completion support and loan guarantees on project debt. We don't ask investors to take risks we haven't taken first.

Economics

Offerings have generally used a Class A / Class B structure with an 8% preferred return and project-specific splits — final terms governed by each investment's operating agreement.

Governance

Manager-managed, project-specific LLCs; a three-principal investment committee; capital-call procedures, major-decision rights, and valuation policy per each operating agreement.

Investor Reporting

Quarterly investor reports, monthly construction updates where appropriate, budget-to-actual and schedule reporting, annual K-1s, and a secure investor portal with source documents.
