## 3. Secondary opportunity — TIC / tenancy in common

Large volume, but read the intent carefully.

| Keyword | Volume | KD% | Note |
|---|---|---|---|
| tenancy in common | 9,900 | 21 | generic US legal-definition intent |
| tenants in common california | 390 | **8** | CA-qualified — worth targeting |
| tenancy in common california | 260 | 20 | CA-qualified — worth targeting |
| tic housing | 70 | 13 | closest to Assemble's actual work |
| tic vs condo | 30 | 0 | good article angle |

**Caution:** the 9,900-volume head term and its variants (`joint tenants vs tenants in common`,
`tenants in common definition`, …) are dominated by estate-planning and title-law definition
queries. Chasing them brings volume that will never convert and dilutes topical focus. **Target
the California-qualified and housing-qualified tail, not the head term.**
