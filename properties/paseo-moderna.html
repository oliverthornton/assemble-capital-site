The Investment

The smallest project in the portfolio, at a 53% margin.

At $0.75M of total project cost, Paseo Moderna is the smallest development in the 21-project schedule — and a useful demonstration that the underwriting discipline does not change with deal size.

The scope was a straightforward ground-up build with no entitlement complexity, designed for an entry-level buyer pool where finish quality and efficient layout matter more than square footage.

It sold in 2016 for $1.14M — $0.40M of gross profit at a 53.3% margin on cost, in line with the portfolio's 51.1% median despite being a fraction of the size of the projects around it.
