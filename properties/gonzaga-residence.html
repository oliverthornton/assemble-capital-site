The Investment

An Assemble Capital project, complete and on the market in Westchester.

8404 Gonzaga is held in Assemble Capital LLC — one of the seven active projects on the current portfolio, and the first of them to reach completion.

Westchester sits in the path of steady owner-user demand: proximity to the Westside and LAX employment, a deep buyer pool at the upper end of the local market, and a housing stock old enough that a well-executed full remodel stands clearly apart from its comparable set. The scope was a comprehensive remodel delivering five bedrooms and five bathrooms across 2,780 square feet.

The home is complete and listed on the open market. It was underwritten to today's closed comparables before acquisition rather than to future appreciation — the discipline that carries across every project. Deal-level economics for this active offering are shared privately with prospective investors.
